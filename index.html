Gnosis Advisory is a boutique consulting firm founded by Eduardo Rios, PhD, in 2025. With over seven years of experience in CEO and board advisory across 120+ global projects, Eduardo has supported private equity clients with commercial due diligence across 66+ transactions totalling several billions in deal value for firms including Advent International, Bain Capital, and Eurazeo.

He has also worked with corporate clients on growth and expansion projects for companies including Google, LinkedIn, DHL, and Accenture.

Gnosis provides strategic analysis using a range of methodologies, from modelling using SQL, R, and Python to qualitative research based on over 2,000 expert interviews, delivering data-driven insights across payments, fintech, retail, and industrial sectors.

Languages: Native fluency in English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese
